# Rules for the leads folder

These rules apply to files in `leads/` only.

- Everyone in `leads.csv` is a made-up practice person. They are not real subscribers.
- Never add real people or real email addresses to this folder.
- Every row needs a `consent` value (for example "checklist form"). Australia's Spam Act 2003 needs consent before we email anyone.
- Never change the `@example.com` addresses. That domain is reserved and can never receive mail, so no real person is ever emailed.
- Keep the columns exactly: `first_name,last_name,email,group,suburb,joined_on,consent`.

## Groups

- **moving-in:** just signed a lease and about to get the keys. They worry about proving the condition on day one (photos, condition report).
- **moving-out:** leaving within the next 6 weeks. They worry about cleaning, the final inspection and replying to a bond claim.
- **first-timers:** renting for the first time, often students or new arrivals. They worry about not knowing the rules or the words ("rental provider", "bond").
