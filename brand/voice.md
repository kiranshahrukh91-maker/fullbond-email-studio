# Fullbond brand voice

Fullbond helps Victorian renters feel calm and prepared about their bond. Our readers are anyone who rents: students, families, first-timers, people moving in and people moving out. Their biggest worry is losing their bond. Every email should leave them feeling looked after and stress-free.

## 1. Our voice in three words

**Warm. Calm. Practical.**

- **Warm:** we talk like a friendly helper, not a company. We're on the renter's side.
- **Calm:** we never scare people. We take the stress out of the situation.
- **Practical:** every email gives a clear next step. "Here's what to do."

## 2. Words we use

Phrases that sound like us (most used first):

- "Don't stress"
- "Here's what to do"
- "You've got this"
- "Here's the plan"
- "Easy fix"
- "Let's sort it"
- "Let's get your bond sorted together"
- "Here's how we can help"

Plain words we prefer:

- "rental provider" (not "landlord" or "lessor")
- "bond" (not "security deposit" or legal wording)
- Short sentences and everyday words, in plain Australian English (organise, colour).

## 3. Words we avoid

- **Scary words or sentences.** For example "you'll lose everything" or "your bond is at risk". Describe the problem calmly and go straight to what to do.
- **Slang.** Keep it friendly but not casual-street. No "mate", "no worries" or similar.
- **Legal jargon.** Use plain words. If a legal term is unavoidable, explain it in one short line.
- **Pressure tactics.** No "act now", "don't miss out", "hurry" or countdown-style panic.
- **Fake reviews or testimonials.** See section 4.

## 4. Claims we must never make

- Never guarantee that anyone will get their full bond back.
- Never present anything as legal advice. Fullbond gives general information only. Say "General information, not legal advice" wherever an email touches on bond, tenancy or legal rights.
- Never use fake urgency or scarcity. The LAUNCH10 offer must really end when we say it does.
- Never use reviews or testimonials we don't really have.
- Never say our kits are approved by the government or the RTBA (Residential Tenancies Bond Authority).
- Never promise a refund beyond the 14-day money-back promise.
- Never promise results. Don't say "this will win your bond dispute" or "you'll definitely get your bond back". The kits help renters prepare. They can't control the outcome.
- Never make claims about other people's behaviour. Don't say "rental providers always try to keep your bond" or "agents can't be trusted". It's scary, and it isn't fair.

## 5. How we sign off

Every email ends with:

> Here to help,
> The Fullbond Team

Below the sign-off, every email must also have the footer required by the Spam Act 2003: the Fullbond name, the contact address (hello@fullbond.com.au) and an unsubscribe link.
