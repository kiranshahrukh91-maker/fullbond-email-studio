## 1. Campaign overview

- **Name:** Bond Ready: the Fullbond launch
- **One-sentence summary:** Welcome every person who downloads the free "10 Bond Mistakes" checklist with five calm, useful emails that show how the Melbourne Renter Toolkit helps them get ready, with a launch code for the first two weeks.
- **Primary objective:** **100 Melbourne Renter Toolkit sales** (any of the three kits) from checklist sign-ups between **Mon 7 Dec 2026 and Sun 3 Jan 2027 (11:59pm AEDT)**. That is the first four weeks after launch.
- **Secondary objectives:**
  - Grow the checklist list with free social posts, renter groups and website guides.
  - Collect real, honest reviews from buyers (no fake or invented reviews).
  - Learn which email and which group converts best, so we can improve it.

## 2. Target audience

Everyone who downloads the free checklist, in three groups.

| Group | Who they are | What they worry about | Buying stage | Likely best-fit kit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| moving-in | Just signed a lease, about to get the keys | Proving the condition on day one (photos, condition report) | Consideration, high intent (time-sensitive) | Move-In Kit $12, or Complete Toolkit $29 |
| moving-out | Leaving within 6 weeks | Cleaning, the final inspection, replying to a bond claim | Decision, high intent | Move-Out Kit $15, or Complete Toolkit $29 |
| first-timers | Renting for the first time, often students or new arrivals | Not knowing the rules or the words ("rental provider", "bond") | Awareness to consideration | Complete Toolkit $29 |

- **Where they spend time:** Instagram, TikTok and Facebook (including Melbourne renter and student groups), search ("how to get my bond back Victoria", "move-out cleaning checklist"), and word of mouth.
- **Shared motivation:** feel calm and prepared about their bond. Every email should leave them feeling looked after.
- **Missing group value:** treated as first-timers.

## 3. Key messages

**Core message:** Don't stress. Here's what to do to get ready for your bond, step by step.

**Supporting messages and proof points**

| Message | Proof point we can honestly use |
|---|---|
| Small steps early make things easier later. | The free checklist, and the move-in photo tip (Day 2). |
| The toolkit gives you a clear plan for where you are right now. | Preview images and contents of each kit (Day 4). |
| It's low risk to try. | 14-day money-back promise, instant download, made for Victorian renters. |
| Launch pricing is real and short. | LAUNCH10: 10% off for the first 2 weeks. It must really end on the date we state. |

**Channel tone notes:** email is warm and short (150 words or fewer in the body). Social posts are helpful first and mention Fullbond last. Renter-group posts must follow each group's own rules.

**Always-on guardrails** (from `brand/voice.md`):
- No promise of a full bond back, no promise of results, no legal advice. Say "General information, not legal advice" wherever an email touches bond, tenancy or legal rights.
- No claim that the kits are approved by the government or the RTBA.
- No claims about how rental providers or agents behave.
- No reviews or testimonials we don't really have.
- Use "rental provider" and "bond". No slang. Real deadlines only.

## 5. Email programme

All send times are in Melbourne time (AEDT). Day 0 is the day of sign-up. Every email carries the Fullbond name, `hello@fullbond.com.au`, an unsubscribe link and the line "Fullbond provides general information, not legal advice." Every email ends with the sign-off "Here to help, The Fullbond Team".

### 5.1 The five welcome emails (from the build plan, Step 6)

| # | Send time | Subject (build plan) | Audience | Purpose | Main link |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Straight away | Your free bond checklist is here | All groups | Deliver the checklist and one quick tip. | Download your checklist |
| 2 | Day 2 | The photo mistake that costs renters the most | All groups, with a group-specific opening | How to take move-in photos that count as evidence. | Get the toolkit, $29 (soft) |
| 3 | Day 4 | What's inside the Fullbond toolkit | All groups, with a group-specific example | Preview images, who it helps, link to buy. | Get the toolkit, $29 |

| 5 | Day 9 | Last reminder: your code ends tonight | All groups | Short, friendly, one button. | Get the toolkit, $29 |

Group angle for each email (the personalise step writes these):

- **moving-out:** cleaning, the final inspection and replying to a bond claim; Move-Out Kit $15 or Complete Toolkit $29.
- **first-timers:** plain-word explanations of "bond" and "rental provider"; Complete Toolkit $29.

Each email has one main link (the Complete Toolkit page). The individual kits are named in the text and sit on the same toolkit page.

### 5.2 Buyer thank-you email

| Send time | Audience | Purpose | Subject |
|---|---|---|---|
| 7 days after purchase | Buyers of any kit | Thank them, check the download worked, and ask for a short, honest review. | To write (50 characters or fewer) |

Rules for this email:
- Ask for an honest review only. No reward and no wording that steers them toward five stars.
- It falls inside the 14-day money-back window, so mention in one calm line that they can reply to hello@fullbond.com.au if the kit isn't right. Don't promise anything beyond the 14-day promise.
- Quote nothing as a testimonial until a real buyer has written it.
- Skip it for anyone who has been refunded.

### 5.3 Timing for rolling sign-ups (this is where LAUNCH10 gets tricky)

LAUNCH10 runs for the first 2 weeks: **Mon 7 Dec 2026 to Sun 20 Dec 2026, ending 11:59pm AEDT** (14 days). I checked the dates by calculation: 7 Dec 2026 is a Monday, 20 Dec 2026 is a Sunday, and 3 Jan 2027 is a Sunday.

| Sign-up date | Day 6 email lands | Day 9 email lands | What they get |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mon 7 Dec | Sun 13 Dec | Wed 16 Dec | Day 6 with code. Day 9 says the code ends Sunday. |
| Tue 8 to Thu 10 Dec | Mon 14 to Wed 16 Dec | Thu 17 to Sat 19 Dec | Day 6 with code. Day 9 says the code ends Sunday. |
| **Fri 11 Dec** | Thu 17 Dec | **Sun 20 Dec** | Day 9 "ends tonight" is true only for this day. |
| Sat 12 to Mon 14 Dec | Fri 18 to Sun 20 Dec | Mon 21 to Wed 23 Dec | Day 6 with code. Day 9 has no code. |
| Tue 15 Dec onwards | After 20 Dec | After 20 Dec | No-code Day 6 and Day 9 (money-back promise, which kit fits). |

**Variants to write** (needs your confirmation, see decision 1):
- **Day 6 (with code) / Day 6 (no code):** same structure, the second leaves out LAUNCH10.
- **Day 9 (ends tonight)** sent 20 Dec only; **Day 9 (ends Sunday)** sent 16 to 19 Dec, with the subject "Last reminder: your code ends Sunday"; **Day 9 (no code)** sent from 21 Dec.
- The code end date and time must match Payhip exactly. Confirm how Payhip handles the end time and that LAUNCH10 applies to all three kits.

For reference, 10% off works out to: Complete Toolkit $26.10, Move-In Kit $10.80, Move-Out Kit $13.50. Confirm the checkout shows the same.

### 5.4 Flow rules

- **Exit the welcome series when:** the person buys (they move to the buyer flow), unsubscribes, bounces or reports spam.
- **A buyer who purchases between emails** gets no further sales emails.
- **No email containing the buy link goes out before 9:00am Mon 7 Dec 2026** (decision 9).
- **Unsubscribes are honoured straight away** and checked each week. (`notes/skills-i-chose.md` says to confirm the Spam Act timing against the ACMA site; do that before go-live.)
- **Don't send to anyone without a recorded consent.** Every contact needs a consent source (for example "checklist form").
- **Opens are unreliable** (mail apps pre-load images), so judge email performance by clicks and sales.
- **The automation tool** (Resend features or a small scheduled script, with Payhip purchase data) is decided in the sending step. This plan does not depend on it.

## 7. Success metrics and funnel maths

### Primary KPI

**100 toolkit sales (any kit) from checklist sign-ups by 11:59pm Sun 3 Jan 2027.**

### What the goal needs (my assumption, not Fullbond data)

Buying rates for a free-to-paid email funnel vary a lot. The table below is a planning guide only. Replace it with real numbers after week 1.

| Sign-up to purchase rate | Sign-ups needed for 100 sales |
|---|---|
| 2% | about 5,000 |
| 3% | about 3,300 |
| 4% | about 2,500 |
| 5% | 2,000 |

If the list is much smaller than this, 100 sales in four weeks will be a stretch with no paid ads. Tell me the list size and I will set a realistic stretch target, a "good" target and a "minimum" target.

Revenue guide: if about 60% of buyers choose the Complete Toolkit and the rest split between the two single kits, the average sale is about $22 before LAUNCH10. So 100 sales is roughly $2,000 to $2,300. This is an assumption.

### Secondary KPIs

| KPI | How we track it | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| New checklist sign-ups (by source and group) | Resend contacts, UTM links on social posts | Needed for the maths above |
| Click rate to the toolkit page, per email | Resend click reports | Main email health measure |
| Checkout conversion (visits to sales) | Payhip, site analytics | Shows if the page needs work |
| LAUNCH10 uses | Payhip coupon report | Compare with sales without a code |
| Unsubscribe rate per email | Resend | Aim for under 0.5% per email |
| Spam complaint rate | Resend | Aim for under 0.1% |
| Refund requests within 14 days | Payhip, hello@ inbox | Early warning on expectations |
| Reviews received | Review replies, site | Real reviews only |

**Attribution:** Payhip does not know who came from the checklist. Match buyer emails from Payhip orders to Resend contacts, and add UTM tags to the toolkit link in each email.

**Reporting:** weekly on Mondays (14 Dec, 21 Dec, 28 Dec) and a final report on Mon 4 Jan 2027.

## 9. Risks and mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| **List is too small for 100 sales.** | Confirm list size now; build a sign-up push (social, groups, guides); set minimum and stretch targets; report weekly. |
| **A new sending domain lands in spam.** | Set up SPF, DKIM and DMARC; start with a small send; keep emails short with one link; monitor bounces and complaints. |
| **Code deadline mismatch** (emails that mention a code after it ended, or "tonight" on the wrong day). | Date-based email variants (section 5.3); test with practice leads set to each date; set Payhip to end the code at the stated time. |
| **Claims drift beyond what we can support.** | Run `brand-review` on every email and post; no promise of results; no testimonials until real ones exist. |
| **The Christmas and New Year period lowers attention.** | Keep emails short and useful; schedule everything in advance; keep support checked over the break. |
| **Renter groups remove posts that look like adverts.** | Read each group's rules; lead with help; mention Fullbond at the end only; ask an admin first if unsure. |
| **Can't tell which sales came from the series.** | UTM links, matching Payhip emails to Resend contacts, and the LAUNCH10 report. |
