# Fullbond Email Studio

## What this studio is for

This studio is for planning and writing email campaigns for Fullbond (fullbond.com.au). Fullbond sells the Melbourne Renter Toolkit:

- Move-In Kit: $12
- Move-Out Kit: $15
- Complete Toolkit: $25

Fullbond grows its email list with a free checklist, "10 Bond Mistakes".

Email is sent through Resend. Checkout is on Payhip. Campaigns are planned and written with the campaign skills in the marketing plugin (marketing@knowledge-work-plugins), such as `campaign-plan` and `email-sequence`.

## Source of truth

`notes/fullbond-build-plan.pdf` is the source of truth for products, prices, pages and the welcome series. If anything here or in a draft disagrees with it, the build plan wins. Tell me about the mismatch.

## Folders

- `brand/` – Fullbond's voice, tone, logo notes and style guidance.
- `leads/` – Notes about the list and lead magnet. No real subscriber data should be committed.
- `campaigns/` – Campaign plans and email drafts, one subfolder per campaign.
- `notes/` – Research, decisions and the build plan (including `skills-i-chose.md`).

## Rules

- Write in plain Australian English (for example "organise", "colour", "bond", "rental provider").
- Never send email to real people. Drafts and tests only, unless I say otherwise in that moment.
- Every email must include the Fullbond name, a contact address and an unsubscribe link (Spam Act 2003).
- Fullbond gives general information, not legal advice. Say so where an email touches on bond, tenancy or legal rights.
- Ask me before deleting anything.
